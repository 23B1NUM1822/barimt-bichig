# How-to guides

A how-to guide helps you finish one task. It assumes LearnLab AI already runs on your computer, as set up in the
[tutorial](../tutorials/first-ai-review.md), and it ends with a way to check that the task worked.

- **[Add a practice problem](add-a-practice-problem.md)**: when you need a new problem for students.
- **[Enable real AI grading](enable-ai-grading.md)**: when you need real AI reviews instead of demo replies.
- **[How to Connect Corg.ly to a Slack Channel](connect-corgly-to-slack.md)**: when you need review results in
  Slack. LearnLab AI cannot do this yet. The guide shows how to confirm that, and what would have to be built.
